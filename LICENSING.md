## What was changed

Each family was modified only in metadata, naming, vertical metrics, outline
format and packaging. No outline was redrawn, with two exceptions. Helix's
Medium, SemiBold and Bold are machine-emboldened from its Regular. And: K24 Bold was
missing the Arabic question mark (U+061F), which was reconstructed by mirroring
the font's own Latin question mark. Original copyright notices are preserved in
the fonts.
