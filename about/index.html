About

Good Kurdish typefaces exist, but they are scattered across forums and file-sharing links, often in broken or mislabelled packages. This is an attempt to collect a few of them somewhere you can actually try them before you download.

What was changed

Each family was modified only in metadata, naming, vertical metrics, outline format and packaging — the things that decide whether a font installs cleanly, shows up under the right name, and holds a stable line height. No outline was redrawn, with one exception: K24 Bold was missing the Arabic question mark, which was reconstructed by mirroring the font's own Latin question mark. Original copyright notices are preserved inside the fonts.

What the site checks

Everything shown here — weights, glyph counts, kerning, character coverage, suggested line height — is read out of the font binaries rather than typed by hand, so the page cannot claim something the fonts do not do. Where a font is missing a character, the specimen page shows it struck through instead of hiding it.

Files

Every family ships as OTF for print, TTF for desktop, and WOFF2 plus WOFF for the web. Downloads and web fonts are served from cdn.daban.one. The full sources, including per-family licence notes, are on GitHub.
